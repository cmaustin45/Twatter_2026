Tip📈 Understanding Consistency

Coefficient of Variation (CV) measures scoring volatility:

  • Lower CV = More consistent (steady scoring each week)
  • Higher CV = Boom/bust team (high variance in scores)

A CV under 15% is very consistent, while over 25% indicates high volatility.
